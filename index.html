Due Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Due Tracker ("the app") helps you keep track of activities you pay for, the sessions you attend, and the payments you owe. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the activities, payments, and notes you enter stay on your device and are never sent to us. The free version of the app shows ads, and the advertising provider collects limited device information to serve them, as described below.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, store, or sell the information you enter into the app. The app has no accounts or sign-in and does not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services. The only information collected when you use the app is collected automatically by the advertising provider (see "Advertising" below).

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter, such as activity names, enrollment names, prices, currencies, session dates, and payment status, is stored only in a local database on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes this data, unless you have exported a backup file (see below).

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows reminder notifications about payments that are due. These notifications are created and shown on your device. The app asks for notification permission (and, on Android, listens for the device restart signal so reminders keep working after a reboot). You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Currency conversion (internet use)

The optional currency conversion feature fetches an exchange rate from the public Frankfurter service (frankfurter.dev). When you use it, the app sends only the two currency codes being converted (for example "USD" and "EUR"). No names, amounts, or other data from the app are sent. As with any internet request, that service can see your device's IP address; see its own policy for how it handles requests. The rest of the app works fully offline.

Backups and sharing

The app lets you export your data to a backup file and import one later. The file is created on your device, and you choose where it goes (for example, saving it or sharing it through your device's share menu). We never receive it. If you send it to a cloud service or another person, that is under your control and that service's privacy terms apply. Backup files are not encrypted by the app, so store them somewhere you trust.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults, such as parents managing payments for classes and lessons. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help resolve it. If you enter a child's name in the app, it remains only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (from which approximate location may be inferred),
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, and language), and
  • information about how ads are viewed and interacted with.

This may be used to show personalized ads, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure ad performance and prevent fraud. We do not give the advertising provider the content of your activities, enrollments, prices, or payments. See how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later from within the app.

Third parties

The third-party services the app contacts are Google AdMob (advertising, described above) and the exchange-rate service (only when you use the conversion feature, described above). The app does not include any other advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

Data security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's protections (screen lock, device encryption, and OS updates). We recommend keeping these enabled.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry inside the app, delete a backup file you created, or remove all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app.
